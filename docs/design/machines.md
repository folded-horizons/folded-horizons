# Machines

## Dimensional Breacher (GT multiblock)

The gateway. GUI destinations: **Personal / Team / Lobby**. No key for
your own spaces. Temp Key slot for exploit pockets. Return: a breach
frame in the destination reads the player's recorded return point.
Entry into unstable pockets burns a lot of energy — the price of
poking a hole in space.

## Root Binder (GT singleblock)

The ownership machine, living **inside the team space**; operates on
the inserting player. Three conversions:

- **Merge** (personal → team): only if the team has no root. Registry
  entry and claims transfer to the team UUID. One-way.
- **Purge** (personal → team's space): team already has a root — the
  player's personal cell is demolished, their address re-pointed.
- **Split** (team → personal): a member leaving reclaims a fresh
  personal cell; the team root stays with the team.

**30-second grace window**: confirmed conversions schedule the switch
30 s out; the breacher refuses the affected space meanwhile, the binder
shows a countdown, and the same actor can cancel before expiry.
Claims transfer with the registry so ownership and protection never
diverge.

## LV Dimension-Generation Multiblock

Late-LV, the first "generate a pocket" machine: creates an **AE2
meteorite dimension** — skystone, the four Inscriber Presses, budding
certus. The multiblock is **consumed in the jump** (one machine per
pocket, each pocket costs a full rebuild), and the loot is finite —
a preview of the exploit cycle before exploit dimensions open.

## Trade Post (lobby trading hall)

A lobby wing where players expose their AE2 networks: each owner
places a Trade Post, links it to their network, and configures
standing barter trades (offer ⇄ want, item + amount). Trades execute
**AE2-to-AE2** — items move directly between networks, no chests, no
meeting. Works with small controller-less networks. No currency.
