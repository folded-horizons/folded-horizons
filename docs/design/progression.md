# Progression

## Game start — the walls feed you

The starting cell has no crafting table (2×2 crafting only) and is
sealed by unbreakable walls. The walls ARE the first resource: mining
a wall block **does not break it** — it yields starter resources and
stays in place.

- Wall tiles have a **material mix** — surveying the cell is the first
  task.
- **With a pickaxe, walls give metal**: iron and copper chunks —
  enough to bootstrap steam-age GT inside the cell.
- **Crafting table is craftable, just absent**: 4 planks' worth of wood
  opens normal 3×3 crafting.
- **Wood comes from bonsai pots** (Bonsai Trees 4): the cell contains
  a pot (or the walls yield one); a pot grows a full tree in one
  block — the compaction economy in miniature.
- The wall-yield tables are the tutorial for the pack's rule: *the
  space you are given is the resource you exploit.*

## Digital storage progression

AE2 arrives early and stays simple until HV:

- **LV — simple ME, no controller**: vibration chamber + one ME Drive
  with hand-made discs + ME Terminal. Controller-less networks cap at
  8 channels — a space budget the controller later lifts. Discs are
  the first real storage production chain; finite type slots per disc
  mirror the compaction pressure.
- **LV meteorite dimension** (see the
  [Dim-Gen machine](machines.md#lv-dimension-generation-multiblock))
  supplies the AE2 endgame chain.
- **HV — the network runs the factory**: ME Interface ties storage
  into GT machines (patterns, autocrafting); the ME Controller lifts
  the 8-channel cap. Storage becomes the nervous system of the root.
- The trading hall assumes everyone eventually runs a network; small
  controller-less networks trade fine.

## Exploit cycle

1. **Temp Key** (tiered, EV+) in a breacher opens the next
   unexhausted pocket of that tier at a per-team offset.
2. Entry is energy-expensive.
3. Extract everything the pocket is worth.
4. **Collapse on leave**: the moment no player remains inside, the
   pocket collapses and **cannot be re-entered**. That instance is
   spent; the team moves to the next pocket.

## Unstable dimension look

Exploitable chunks are surrounded by empty space — floating islands
in the void. At the edge of each pocket sits a larger cube of
unbreakable "sky blocks"; from inside it reads as an ordinary horizon —
a clean sky in every direction. Backrooms vibe: an empty, endless
skybox that is actually a wall closing you in. Nothing confirms the
seam until you try to fly, pillar up, or mine the sky.

## Breaking out of the sky (lategame)

At very high tiers, progression allows breaking *through* the sky cube
into the **corrupted space** around the pocket — a still-limited shell
beyond the fake sky, not true infinity. The sky stops being the end of
the world and becomes the last door.

## Expansion

Root/repeatable/special interiors grow with progression: at
configurable tier thresholds the interior bounds grow outward and the
newly exposed band gets fresh wall shell. Expansion is a
**shell-growing operation**, not a teleport.
