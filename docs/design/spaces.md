# Pocket Types

| Type | Persistence | Uniqueness | Collapses? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Root | permanent, expandable | 1 per owner | never |
| Repeatable | permanent | unlimited, even recursive | never |
| Unstable (exploit) | dies when last player leaves | spent on use | always |
| Special (celestial) | permanent | 1 per owner per body | never |

## Root space

One persistent cell per owner (player or team), expandable
([expansion](progression.md#expansion)). Spawn point, safety net,
identity anchor.

## Repeatable space

**Empty space, like the root** — the player fills it however they
want. Its multiblock can be built **multiple times, even recursively**:
one repeatable pocket can host the machine that opens another. As
permanent as root, just not unique. Good for annexes, farms, outposts,
and layouts that outgrow the root without deserving a celestial body.

## Unstable (exploit) pockets

Tiered, opened with Temp Keys at EV+. See the
[exploit cycle](progression.md#exploit-cycle).

## Special dimensions

Pockets of specific celestial bodies (Moon, Mars, Mercury, ...),
tuned to recipes/environments that only make sense there — vacuum
processing, low-g assembly, solar exposure. The signature one: a
**glass wall looking into a black hole** — time/space recipes at the
end of the line.

They behave like root spaces: expandable, persistent, no collapse.
The multiblock is an **address, not a door** — a second copy of the
same special multiblock links into the same pocket instead of
spawning a duplicate.

Unstable pockets are how you *earn*; special dimensions are what you
*become*.
